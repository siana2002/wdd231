Chamber of Commerce Site Plan

Site Name

Melbourne Cham of Commerce

Site Purpose

This website provides resources for businesses in the city of Melbourne. The site will provide information on local events, host networking opportunities and increase local shopping. It aims to promote local economic growth, create a more safe and loving community and support small local businesses.

Target Market

Local business owners in Melbourne and also in neighboring states.

Site Goals

  • Increase visibility and traffic towards small local business owners
  • Attract new businesses and encourage economic growth within the community
  • Encourage all business owners, local and outer, to invest in creating a better community

User Personas

  • Small Business Owner: Mary is a 40 year old entrepreneur who owns a small event planning business in town. Her business is not getting any visibility and is in need of marketing support and business resources to increase traffic to her store.
  • The CEO:An internationally known marketing company has expressed interest in helping small business owners in Australia put their names out no only to their states but to the world. The CEO has been tasked with completing this task. The company is interested in partnership opportunities
  • The New Residents: Jessica recently moved into the community with her spouse and both planning to get married. They are looking for businesses to help them plan for their wedding.

Scenarios

  • A local business owner is interested to join a local chamber of commerce to network with other local business owners. They join the commerce for help on how to increase visibility and traffic towards his company.
  • A couple find a small business in Melbourne that can create their dream wedding. When flying in they love the area and want to move in and help out more in the community. They search the chambers of commerce website for upcoming local events and charities.
  • A visiting CEO from an international marketing company is looking to promote local small businesses in Melbourne. He visits the chambers website for upcoming events for companies that will positively impact the community.

SEO Plan

  • Use relevant keywords in site descriptions, content, and blog posts.
  • Verify the site on Googles Business Profile
  • Get inbound links or backlinks from all member business to improve rankings
  • Embed Google Analytics in all site pages
